# ExtractMint Desktop

## Your documents stay on your computer.

ExtractMint Desktop is a local-first Mac and Windows app that reuses ExtractMint’s conversion, review, and export workflow. Core document processing runs on your computer.

Convert bank statements, receipts, invoices, and office documents, review extracted rows, and export clean working files.

## Availability

Signed desktop downloads are being prepared. No desktop installer is available yet. Signed Mac and Windows installers will appear on the [desktop page](https://extractmint.iangoh.com/desktop/) after publication.
