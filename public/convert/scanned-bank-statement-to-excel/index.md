# Scanned Bank Statement to Excel

> ExtractMint can use OCR to convert cleaner statement scans and images into review-ready spreadsheet exports.

## Use Case

Use this workflow when the statement is an image, scan, or PDF with little embedded text. ExtractMint can run OCR in the browser and then structure the resulting text into rows.

## Best Inputs

- Straight, high-resolution scans.
- Clear dates, descriptions, and amount columns.
- Pages without heavy shadows, skew, or handwriting.
- Files where opening and closing balances are visible.

## Review Requirements

Scanned statements are less reliable than text PDFs. Always check dates, descriptions, withdrawal and deposit placement, page breaks, and balance movement before using the exported file.

## Outputs

OCR-based conversions can be exported to Excel XLSX, CSV, DOCX, PDF, review JSON, review-pack ZIP, and combined workbook formats.
