Should I use QuickBooks CSV or QBO?

CSV is simple and widely supported. QBO (OFX) includes more structure and can be more reliable. ExtractMint supports both so you can pick what works for your account.

Will QuickBooks accept any date format?

Slash dates like 03/05/2026 can be ambiguous. ExtractMint lets you pick a DD/MM or MM/DD assumption so imports don’t shift.

Does the file leave my computer?

The current static app processes files in the browser. A future SaaS backend should add explicit retention controls and audit logs.
