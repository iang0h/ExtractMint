Why do Xero imports fail or skip rows?

Common causes include mismatched date formats for your Xero region, blank amounts, and duplicate references. ExtractMint surfaces these issues so you can fix them before import.

Does the file leave my computer?

The current static app processes files in the browser. A future SaaS backend should add explicit retention controls and audit logs.
