Bank CSV cleaner

Fix messy bank exports for QuickBooks and Xero.

When your bank CSV has extra columns, odd date formats, or debit/credit split fields, imports get rejected. ExtractMint’s cleaner helps you auto-map columns and export import-ready CSV — local-first in the browser.

Outputs

QuickBooks 3-column CSV, Xero-ready bank CSV, or a cleaned ExtractMint table for Excel exports and review packs.

Local-first: nothing uploads in the current static build.

What the cleaner fixes

  • Extra columns like running balances, categories, notes, or duplicate headers.
  • Date formats that don’t match the target import.
  • Amount fields split into Debit/Credit columns.
  • Currency symbols, commas, or accounting-style negatives (e.g. (12.34)).

QuickBooks (CSV)

Exports a QuickBooks-friendly 3-column CSV: Date, Description, Amount.

  • Auto-map columns from headers + sample rows.
  • Choose a date format when it’s ambiguous.
  • Split large files into chunks when imports have row limits.

Xero (CSV)

Exports a Xero-ready bank statement CSV with Date, Amount, Payee, Description, and Reference.

  • Normalize dates to reduce import ambiguity.
  • Optionally make references unique to avoid accidental de-dupe.
  • Keep one signed Amount column (deposits positive, withdrawals negative).

ExtractMint table

Creates a cleaned ExtractMint-friendly table you can convert to Excel and review packs.

  • Convert to XLSX, CSV, PDF, DOCX, QBO, and review packs.
  • Balance trail signals carry into reconciliation exports.
  • Use support diagnostics when a bank format is unusual.

How to use it

  1. Open the cleaner at /#bank-csv-cleaner.
  2. Upload a bank-export .csv or .xlsx.
  3. Click Auto-map columns (or map fields manually).
  4. Export the format you need, then import into QuickBooks or Xero.

Tip: if an import fails, try a smaller chunk size and confirm your date format matches the accounting system’s importer.
